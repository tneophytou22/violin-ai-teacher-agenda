# TKTL V1 — Teacher Unit Cards L1–L4

This document records the real Teacher Unit Card registration scope for L1T1 through L4T2. The executable source is `cards-v1.js`.

Each card follows the locked contract: exactly 5 Pure Technical items, 5 Etudes/Studies, and 5 Repertoire options, plus technical intent, prerequisites, scales link, domains, teacher decision logic, readiness criteria, next-term dependency, difficulty band, and evidence status.
